    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions and PowerPyx's roadmap (difficulty 4/10, about 200 hours, one playthrough, no mission replay, free-roam after the story). Work the stages in order; every trophy below is filed under the stage where you earn it.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the whole list is reported as potentially missable. Everything else can be earned after the story in free-roam — PowerPyx is explicit about that, and it is why the platinum needs only one playthrough.

    Save habits that protect the run

    • Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots plus 1 autosave). There is no mission replay.
    • Do Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1, then keep that Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops.
    • Do not use cheats on your trophy save — cheats disable trophies and saving.
    • Optional missions with an hourglass icon are time-sensitive, but they do not matter for trophies: there are far more than the 100 the checklist wants.

    What we do not claim

    • Four hidden trophies — In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin — have no confirmed unlock method at launch. This guide says so plainly instead of guessing.
    • Exact locations beyond what PowerPyx publishes are not restated here; its location guides are linked as sources.
    • Nothing here was played by us: the guides are built from the official descriptions and the cited guides, and they say where a fact comes from.
